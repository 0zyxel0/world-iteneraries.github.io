💡 Travel & Dining Survival Guide
  • Reservations: Book 1 to 3 months prior. Sorn and Potong require booking on exact release dates.
  • Dress Code: Smart Casual to Formal (Long pants, closed shoes required for men in fine dining).
  • Transit: Use BTS / MRT or boats between 5:30 PM – 8:00 PM to avoid Bangkok's heavy road traffic.
